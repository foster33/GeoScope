GeoScope reads the exact coordinates you click — never a place name — so the report always describes this spot, not a town elsewhere that shares its name. Everything inside your circle gets gathered: people, weather past and present, settlements, landmarks.

Saved lookups

Each saved place keeps an immutable timeline of snapshots for its exact spot and radius — press ＋ New snapshot to re-sample it. Stored on this server, so everything survives restarts.

Watching

Each watched area is re-checked automatically, paced by how fast each source moves — news every 30 min, earthquakes & severe weather hourly, air quality every 3 h, slower map data weekly. Differences land here as a change log.

Space

Live orbits above the globe: both space stations and the ~150 brightest satellites, propagated in your browser from CelesTrak orbital elements — positions are real, not animated. Zoom out to see the whole constellation; it hides itself near street level.
